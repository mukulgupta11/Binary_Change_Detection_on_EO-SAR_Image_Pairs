# Time and Resource Log

## Time Breakdown (Total: ~18 Hours)
- Data Exploration & Setup: 2 hours
- Literature Review & Architecture Planning: 3 hours
- Implementation (Dataloaders, Loss, Pipeline): 4 hours
- Training & Hyperparameter Tuning: 7 hours
- Evaluation & Report Writing: 2 hours

## Hardware & Compute
- Machine: Google Colab Free Tier (and Kaggle Kernels)
- GPU: 1x NVIDIA T4 (15 GB VRAM)
- Training Time per Epoch: ~13.5 minutes
- Total Wall-Clock Training Time: ~3.5 hours for 15 epochs

## Constraints & Impact on Decisions
- Memory Constraint: The 15GB VRAM limit on the T4 GPU forced the use of Automatic Mixed Precision (AMP) and limited the batch size to 2 to prevent CUDA OOM errors. It also heavily influenced the decision to use Early Fusion (concatenating 4 channels into one encoder) rather than a memory-heavy Siamese Late Fusion architecture.
- Time Constraint: The 3.5-hour Colab session timeout required reducing the model backbone from ResNet-101 to ResNet-34 and lowering total epochs to 15 to ensure clean termination and weight saving before the quota expired.
